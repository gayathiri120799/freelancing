About FreeVerse

Student talent. Real projects. Global possibilities.

Our Vision

To create a student-powered freelancing ecosystem where talent, creativity and technology come together to solve real-world problems.

Our Mission

FreeVerse provides students with a practical platform to showcase their skills, gain project experience, connect with clients and develop professional confidence.

What We Provide

Students can create professional freelancer profiles, display their skills and projects, discover opportunities and apply for jobs. Clients can create accounts, publish requirements and discover suitable student talent.

Who Can Become a Freelancer?

The freelancer side is designed for eligible students of Kangeyam Institute of Technology with valid KIT registration numbers.

Who Can Become a Client?

Clients can be individuals, startups, companies, organizations, creators or other businesses looking for digital and technical services.
